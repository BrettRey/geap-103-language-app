# My language pack

> Lines that start with > are instructions. The app ignores them. You can delete them.
> Change the title above to your language, for example: # Spanish (Colombia)

language:
language code:
made by:
for:

> language code: a short code for your language, like es for Spanish or ar for Arabic.
>   Your agent can tell you the code. Check its answer.
> made by: your first name or a nickname. Never write your student number.
> for: who will use this pack? For example: classmates who want to talk to my family.

## At a restaurant

> This is a situation. Change it to a situation you know well.
> Write 4 or more phrases for each situation.
> If you work in a group, add "made by:" with your name under your situation's ## line.

phrase:
meaning:
say it:
note:
audio:

> phrase: in your language, written the way you write it.
> meaning: what it means, in English.
> say it: how to say it, using English letters. Use CAPITALS for the strong part, like "to-MOR-oh" for "tomorrow".
> note: when to use it, who to say it to, or a cultural rule.
> audio: the name of your recording, like thank-you.m4a. Put the file in the same folder.
>   You can leave audio empty.
